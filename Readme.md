# Unveiling Profitable Customer Acquisition Channels through CLTV Analysis in Python

## Introduction

The focus of this project sharpens on a critical aspect of business growth and sustainability: **understanding the long-term value generated by our customers and, more specifically, identifying the most profitable channels through which we acquire them.**

Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV) is the estimated total revenue a customer will contribute to our business throughout their entire relationship. By meticulously analyzing CLTV, we can move beyond immediate gains and make strategic decisions about customer acquisition and retention. This project aims to dissect the relationship between the costs invested in acquiring customers through various channels and the long-term revenue these customers generate. Ultimately, our goal is to pinpoint the most cost-effective and high-yield acquisition strategies.

## Data Dictionary

## Data Dictionary

| Column Name        | Data Type | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ------------------ | --------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `CustomerID`       | Object    | Unique identifier for each customer.                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| `AcquisitionChannel` | Object    | The marketing channel through which the customer was acquired (e.g., 'Organic', 'Paid Search', 'Social Media', 'Email Marketing', 'Referral', 'Affiliate').                                                                          |
| `AcquisitionCost`    | Float64   | The cost incurred to acquire a customer through a specific acquisition channel.                                                                                                                                                   |
| `ConversionRate`     | Float64   | The rate at which leads from a particular acquisition channel convert into customers (ranging from 0 to 1).                                                                                                                         |
| `Revenue`            | Float64   | The initial revenue generated by the customer.                                                                                                                                                                                          |


**Specifically, this project seeks to answer the following key questions:**

1.  **What is the average initial revenue generated by customers acquired through each channel?**
2.  **What is the average cost of acquiring a customer through each acquisition channel?**
3.  **Is there a correlation between the conversion rate of a channel and the initial revenue of the customers acquired through it?**
4.  **What is the initial profitability (Revenue - Acquisition Cost) per customer for each acquisition channel?**
5.  **What is the estimated Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV) for customers acquired through different channels (using a simplified estimation)?**
6.  **Which customer acquisition channels are the most profitable based on both initial profitability and estimated long-term value?**

This analysis is particularly crucial as it allows us to:

* **Optimize Marketing Spend:** Direct resources towards channels that not only acquire customers but also deliver the highest lifetime value, maximizing our return on investment.
* **Identify High-Value Customer Sources:** Understand which channels attract customers who are likely to spend more and remain engaged with our business for a longer duration.
* **Evaluate Channel Efficiency:** Quantify the effectiveness of different acquisition channels by comparing their costs, conversion rates, and the resulting customer lifetime value.
* **Inform Strategic Decisions:** Provide data-backed insights to refine our customer acquisition strategies, potentially leading to increased profitability and sustainable growth.

To achieve these objectives, this week's project will involve a focused analysis of a dataset containing information on customer acquisition channels, associated costs, conversion rates, and the revenue generated by these customers. Our methodology will include:

1.  **Data Preparation:** Loading, cleaning, and structuring the provided data for effective analysis.
2.  **Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV) Calculation:** Implementing a relevant CLTV calculation based on the available data (revenue, acquisition cost, and potentially making reasonable assumptions about customer lifetime if not directly provided).
3.  **Channel-Specific CLTV Aggregation:** Calculating the average CLTV for customers acquired through each distinct channel.
4.  **Profitability Assessment:** Evaluating the profitability of each acquisition channel by considering the cost of acquisition and the average CLTV of the customers acquired through that channel. This will likely involve calculating the Return on Investment (ROI) per channel.
5.  **Conversion Rate Analysis:** Examining the relationship between conversion rates and CLTV across different channels to identify high-performing combinations.
6.  **Identification of Optimal Channels:** Determining the most profitable customer acquisition channels based on the CLTV and profitability analysis.
7.  **Actionable Recommendations:** Formulating clear and data-driven recommendations to optimize customer acquisition strategies and improve overall business profitability.

## Key Findings

The analysis reveals the following key insights:

* **Referral and Email Marketing are the most profitable channels.** They consistently deliver high-value customers with strong initial profitability and the highest estimated CLTV. These channels should be prioritized and optimized to maximize customer acquisition and long-term revenue.
* **Organic acquisition is a cost-effective channel** with high customer volume and zero acquisition costs, resulting in good initial profitability. However, it exhibits the lowest CLTV, indicating lower long-term value per customer.
* **Paid Search presents a moderate return** with moderate initial profitability and CLTV. While it can drive traffic, its higher acquisition costs require careful optimization to ensure profitability.
* **Affiliate and Social Media demonstrate the lowest profitability.** Both channels have low initial profitability and CLTV, raising concerns about their cost-effectiveness and long-term value.
